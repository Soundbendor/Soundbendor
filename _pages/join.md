We are actively recruiting students with interdisciplinary backgrounds in music and computer science who want to combine these interests through research in artificial intelligence at Oregon State University.

### Graduate students

- **Computer Science** (M.S., M.Eng., Ph.D.): applicants usually hold an undergraduate degree in computer science or a related field.
- **Artificial Intelligence** (M.S., Ph.D.): applicants have strong programming skills and often come from other backgrounds, such as math, linguistics, psychology or music.

Apply through the [OSU Graduate School](https://gradschool.oregonstate.edu/) and name Dr. Donnelly as a faculty member of interest.

### Undergraduates

OSU undergraduates join the lab through funded research programs (URSA Engage, the Cascades Research Fellowship), Honors College theses and the senior capstone.
Several projects have led to conference papers and talks; see [Projects]({{ '/projects/' | relative_url }}) and [Theses]({{ '/theses/' | relative_url }}).

### Diversity, equity and inclusion

The Soundbendor Lab celebrates diversity in its many forms, perspectives and experiences.
We support all who may think, feel, hear and sing life a little differently, in a collaborative environment where all voices are heard and valued.
