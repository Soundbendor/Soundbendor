AURA: Audio Understanding and Research Annotation

Madison Neiswonger, Dylan DeForrest, Hunter Scholtz
Audio TaggingDatasetsInterfaces
In progressSlides
Read more

AURA (Audio Understanding and Research Annotation) is a web tool for crowdsourcing labels for audio datasets. Participants play sounds and label them in eight modes, from Best Match to a two-dimensional emotion grid and labels over time; researchers manage studies, upload audio, build label sets, view analytics and download data.

Can ChatGPT judge how a song feels? GPT-4o-mini rated the valence and arousal of Billboard Top 100 songs since 1958 using three prompt styles: a direct number, five descriptive words and a paragraph. Timbral Analysis

Alec Krsek
Audio TaggingTimbreSignal Processing
Poster
Read more

A Python tool that labels any sound file with timbre words. It extracts 15 acoustic features with librosa and Praat, such as spectral centroid, jitter and shimmer, and maps them to descriptors like strident or wavering. Tested on ESC-50, it aims to label audio datasets automatically.

Hierarchical Spectral Clustering

Matthew O'Malley-Nichols
TimbreRhythmSignal Processing
VideoPoster
Watch and read more

A hierarchical clustering algorithm that divides songs into segments of similar timbre and approximate length, using recurrence and similarity matrices built from individual beats. Tested on rock, electronic, hip-hop and mixed-genre songs, it balances segment-length accuracy and cohesion better than k-means, helping synchronize events with a song's energy.
