Bridging the Timbre Gap: A Generative Approach to Audio Labeling

Marc Vannucci
Music TaggingTimbreAudio Synthesis
VideoSlidesPoster
Watch and read more

A synthetic dataset for classifying subjective timbre, the tone color that separates a guitar from a piano. Gemini writes varied sound scenarios for descriptive tags such as hollow, warm or metallic, a validator removes near-duplicates, and ElevenLabs generates the audio, avoiding costly and inconsistent manual labeling.

Timbral Analysis

Alec Krsek
Music TaggingTimbreSignal ProcessingTools
Poster
Read more

A Python tool that labels any sound file with timbre words. It extracts 15 acoustic features with librosa and Praat, such as spectral centroid, jitter and shimmer, and maps them to descriptors like strident or wavering. Tested on ESC-50, it aims to label audio datasets automatically.

Hierarchical Spectral Clustering

Matthew O'Malley-Nichols
Signal Processing
VideoPoster
Watch and read more

A hierarchical clustering algorithm that divides songs into segments of similar timbre and approximate length, using recurrence and similarity matrices built from individual beats. Tested on rock, electronic, hip-hop and mixed-genre songs, it balances segment-length accuracy and cohesion better than k-means, helping synchronize events with a song's energy.

Poster

A text-to-speech tool that voices constructed languages, which have no native speakers or recordings.
