Join

We are actively recruiting students with interdisciplinary backgrounds in music and computer science who want to combine these interests through research in artificial intelligence. Our students work on projects in music emotion, timbre, music theory, speech, bioacoustics, food waste and more; many have co-authored papers, written theses and presented at national and international conferences, from NCUR to EvoMUSART, ICMI and ICNLSP.

Diversity, equity and inclusion

The Soundbendor Lab celebrates diversity in its many forms, perspectives and experiences. We support all who may think, feel, hear and sing life a little differently, in a collaborative environment where all voices are heard and valued.
