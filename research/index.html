Music emotion

Can machines estimate how a piece of music makes people feel?

We model the emotions listeners hear in music, from audio features and from the words people use about music: social media discussions, concert program notes and descriptions generated by large language models. Because emotion-labelled music is scarce, much of this work builds new datasets.

Timbre and musical structure

What do machine learning models understand about timbre, rhythm and music theory?

We study how models represent the qualities of sound and the structure of music: classifying timbre from text alone, probing generative music models for music-theory concepts, measuring rhythm, and labelling sounds with the words people use for timbre.

Papers

  • Probing for Advanced Music Theory Concepts in Generative Music Models Kwan & Donnelly 2026
  • Toward Better Machine Understanding of Rhythm: Rhythmicness as a Regression Problem Tassin & Donnelly 2026
  • Transposition of Simple Waveforms from Raw Audio with Deep Learning Donnelly & Carlson 2023

Machine listening for animals, health and the environment

Can sound and sensor data help us monitor wildlife, rivers and animal health?

We apply signal processing and machine learning to sounds and sensor signals outside music: harbor porpoise echolocation clicks, bird song, river levels and seizures in dogs wearing a smart collar.

The smart compost bin

How much food do households really throw away, and what is it?

Our AI-enabled compost bin weighs and photographs kitchen waste and lets people describe it out loud, with a companion app that shows households their waste. Funded by the Foundation for Food & Agriculture Research and the Kroger Co. Zero Hunger | Zero Waste Foundation, it will collect a large new dataset of household food waste images.

Datasets & code

Public data released with our papers. Each links to its paper for citation.

Dataset SynTheory+ on Hugging Face · MIT license

Synthetic 4-second WAV examples (from generated MIDI) of polyrhythms, dynamics, seventh chords, mode mixture and secondary dominants, for probing what generative music models such as MusicGen and Jukebox encode about music theory.

Cite: Probing for Advanced Music Theory Concepts in Generative Music Models (Kwan & Donnelly 2026)
Dataset EmotioNotes on GitHub

Historical New York Philharmonic concert program notes (from the 1840s on), with performance metadata and estimated valence, arousal and dominance scores, for music emotion recognition and cultural musicology.

Cite: EmotioNotes Dataset: Decoding Emotions in Classical Music Through Concert Program Notes (Khanal & Donnelly 2025)
