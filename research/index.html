Research

Music Emotion

We study the emotions people hear in music and the feelings music evokes. We use audio, listener ratings, and written sources such as concert program notes to build and evaluate models of musical emotion. This work also informs playlists that guide listeners through changes in mood.

Sound Perception

We study how people hear and describe sound, from differences in timbre to the effects of hearing loss. We compare listener judgments with model predictions, use text to train audio classifiers, and build interactive tools that let people explore how hearing loss can change familiar sounds.

TimbrePerceptionHearing

Musical Structure

We study how computers recognize, represent, and generate musical structure. We test what generative models learn about rhythm and harmony, build systems that transcribe piano recordings into notes, and create datasets to test these abilities. Our musicology work examines music in its historical context.

MusicologyTranscriptionGenerationExplore SynTheory+

Applied Sensing

We use sound, images, and other sensor data to measure changes that can be difficult to observe directly. Our projects detect wildlife calls and seizures in dogs, forecast river levels, and document household food waste. The binsight.ai smart compost bin combines measurements, images, and a companion app.
