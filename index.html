Soundbendor Lab

Machine understanding of sound and music

The Soundbendor Lab is a research group in the School of Electrical Engineering and Computer Science at Oregon State University. We use artificial intelligence and deep learning to study sound and music, drawing on computer science, signal processing, and musicology. Our interdisciplinary research explores computational musicology, music emotion recognition, acoustic analysis, and bioacoustics, engaging undergraduate and graduate students in Corvallis, Bend, and on Ecampus.
